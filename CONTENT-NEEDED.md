# Content needed from The Spa & Studio at MDS

A homepage preview built from what is publicly on thespaandstudioatmds.com as of
2026-09-05. Nothing on the page is invented. Below is everything unconfirmed, left
out, or worth a decision before this goes anywhere near their domain.

## Things on their current site they will want to know about

- **The browser tab says "Welcome."** Their `<title>` is `Welcome` — the untouched
  GoDaddy Website Builder default. That is the text in the browser tab and the blue
  headline in every Google result. Fixed here.
- **An unfilled award badge is live on their site.** One of the images in their gallery
  is a The Knot "Best of Weddings" template that still reads **"Your Business Name Here"**
  where the salon name should be. It is not used on this preview. Worth them pulling down
  regardless of what happens with us.
- **No footer copyright at all.** Added here, computed at build time so it cannot go stale.
- **Hair services are filed under "The Nail Lounge"** on their own services page, below the
  manicure and pedicure lists, while the site's tagline is "a luxury day spa & hair studio."
  The preview follows their grouping exactly. Ask whether that is intended — hair probably
  deserves its own heading.

## Unverified, so not used anywhere on the page

- **Review counts.** The outreach tracker notes 51 WeddingWire reviews and Yelp photos.
  Real, but they drift and we do not control them, so no count or star rating is printed.
  The awards named on the page come from their own team-page copy instead.
- **Anything about a specific staff member other than Lyndsie.** Their team page lists
  around sixteen people with bios. The homepage summarises the team rather than naming
  them, so nobody is described wrongly and nobody is left out.

## Photography

Four images are reused from their own site: the wash area with the tan leather chairs
(the strongest photograph they have, used for the hero), the waiting lounge with the
fireplace, a styling station, and the studio downstairs. Plus a shot of the front desk.

What is missing: **a photograph of a treatment room.** The spa's three treatment rooms are
the thing the copy leans on hardest and there is no picture of one anywhere on their site.
Also worth asking for: a clean shot of the nail lounge, and a current bridal gallery.

## Booking

They run on **Phorest**. The preview does not try to replace it — "Book online" and the
gift-card button link straight out to their existing Phorest pages. That is deliberate:
their booking works, the website around it is the problem.
